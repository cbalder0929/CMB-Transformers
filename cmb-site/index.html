Why it’s free

No sales pitch.
Just an honest look.

I don’t believe personal training should start with a sales pitch. The first session is free because I want us to figure out two things:

  • Can I genuinely help you reach your goals?
  • Do we work well together?

If the answer is yes, great — we’ll build a plan. If not, you’ll still leave with a better understanding of your fitness.

What happens in your first session

One hour.
Three steps.

  1. 1

    We get to know each other

    We’ll spend time talking about your goals, what’s held you back in the past, and what success actually looks like for you. No judgment. Just an honest conversation.

  2. 2

    We train

    This isn’t a consultation where we just sit and talk. You’ll get a real workout so I can see how you move, coach you through exercises, and give you immediate feedback.

  3. 3

    We build your roadmap

    At the end of the session, I’ll explain exactly what I think you should focus on first and how I’d approach your training. Even if you decide not to continue, you’ll leave with direction instead of more confusion.
