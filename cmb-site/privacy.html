Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What I collect

When you book a free session through the scheduler on this site, you give your name, email address, and anything you choose to tell me about your goals, training history, or injuries. That booking is created in Google Calendar, which is where I read it from.

What I use it for

To schedule your session, send you confirmations and reminders, and follow up afterward. That’s all.

Who else sees your data

Booking is handled entirely by Google Calendar’s appointment scheduling. Google processes your booking details on my behalf and is subject to its own privacy policy. This site itself has no database and no server — it stores nothing about you. I don’t sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for marketing purposes, ever.

Analytics and tracking

This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. Google may set cookies inside the embedded scheduler in order to run it.

How long I keep it

Calendar records are kept while you’re a client and for a reasonable period after, so I have training history if you come back. Ask me to delete yours and I will.
